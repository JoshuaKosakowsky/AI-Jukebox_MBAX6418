How playback works

Qwen writes a short introduction for each queued song; Chatterbox speaks it with your selected DJ voice or reference, then the music plays — speech and music never overlap. Pause and Skip work during speech and music, and the queue advances on its own after each song. Reloading the page keeps the queue and never generates new speech.
