Snow boots heavy on the linoleum floor
I tuck my crown behind the office door
Coffee steam rises in the cold gray light
Trying to blend in, keeping it tight
The boss calls a meeting, I stand up straight
Praying my antlers don’t meet his fate
A gentle nod from Sarah down the hall
She sees the moose, not just the wall
We laugh at jokes that only we understand
In this small town across the land
No hunters here, just hearts beating true
My place is here, and so are you
The winter fades with a salsa beat
Warm brass swells beneath my feet
I am home, finally seen
